Regular Meetings

The Fraser Valley Stamp Club meets on the second Tuesday of each month, except July and August, from 6:00 to 9:00 PM in the school library at W.J. Mouat Secondary School, 32355 Mouat Drive, Abbotsford, BC.

Upcoming meetings

  • Second Tuesday of each month except July and August, 6:00 PM.

What happens at a meeting

There is active trading throughout, as well as a silent auction and a buy board with lots submitted by members. People start arriving at 6:00 PM, the table (silent) auction closes at 7:30 PM, and a short business meeting follows.

At the November meeting there is an annual call auction with roughly 100 lots, ranging in value from $5 to $250.

Visitors are welcome – come along and introduce yourself, or join the club for $10 a year.
