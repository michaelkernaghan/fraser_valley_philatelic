Sea & Valley 2027 - Philatelic Exhibition

Presented by the Fraser Valley Stamp Club and the Peace Arch Stamp Club

Location: West Langley Hall, 9402 - 208 St., Langley, BC

Date: Saturday, April 17th, 2027

Hours: 9:00 am - 3:00 pm

Admission: Toonie ($2) per person (children 12 and under FREE)

A great day for collectors of all ages and interests. Discover, trade, learn and connect.

  • 10 dealer bourse - stamps, covers, collections and supplies
  • Bid & Bin (Buy It Now) boards - great finds for every collector
  • Two mystery box raffle draws
  • Free on-site parking

Info: peacearchstampclub.com

The Vancouver Coins, Stamps & Collectibles Show

Location: Nikkei National Museum & Cultural Centre, 6688 Southoaks Crescent, Burnaby

This show will feature 40 tables of coins, stamps, and collectables. Buy, sell, or trade items such as Canadian coins, world coins, ancient coins, stamps, art, covers, postcards, medals, tokens, bank notes, and more. Free coins for kids and free appraisals are available.

Show Dates for 2026:

  • Saturday, September 12th, 2026
  • Saturday, November 21st, 2026

Show Dates for 2027:

  • Saturday, September 11th, 2027
  • Saturday, November 13th, 2027

Hours: Saturdays, 9:30 am - 3:30 pm

Admission: $3 per person (under age 16 FREE)

Please stay home if you are not feeling well.
